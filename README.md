## What is Artificial Intelligence?

Artificial Intelligence, also known as AI, is a field of computer science that tries to create machines or programs that can perform tasks that usually require human intelligence.

Examples of AI include:

- Chatbots
- Machine translation
- Speech recognition
- Self-driving cars
- Image recognition
- Recommendation systems

## Why AI is Important

AI is important because it helps people solve problems faster and more efficiently. It is used in education, medicine, business, transportation, and entertainment.
